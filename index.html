About Me

I am Krish Mehta, a first-year B.Tech IT student at RMK Engineering College, Chennai. I hail from Shimla district, Himachal Pradesh, and have a strong background in academics and extracurricular activities.

I completed my early schooling at Saraswati Vidya Mandir High School, Baragaon, in Kumarsain Tehsil, and later attended Gurukul International School, Solan for my 8th grade. From 9th to 12th grade, I studied at MRA DAV Public School, Solan, where I was honored to serve as the Head Boy during my final year. I excelled in various competitions, winning medals in sports, quizzes, and events like kabaddi and relay races at the block and state levels.

In 12th grade, I achieved 82% and completed my Informatics Practices project on the Olympics medal tally. My academic journey has been complemented by my involvement in leadership roles and a deep interest in skill development. I scored 89% in 10th and have continued working on a variety of projects during my B.Tech.
